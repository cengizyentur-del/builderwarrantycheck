What is Home Watch?

Your home, checked on schedule while you're gone

Home Watch is a visiting service for empty homes. We walk your home on a set schedule, run the checks below, and document everything with photos — so a small issue never gets two months to grow.

  • ✓Catch water early. The #1 risk in an empty home: a supply line, water heater or AC drain leaking for weeks.
  • ✓Keep systems alive. Faucets run and toilets flushed keep traps sealed and plumbing healthy; the AC keeps humidity — and mold — down.
  • ✓Deter trouble. Mail collected, lights right, doors and windows verified — your home looks lived in, not vacant.
  • ✓Documented visits. Many insurance policies limit coverage on homes left vacant for extended periods. Regular dated, photo-documented visits show your home was being looked after.

Questions

Home Watch FAQ

What happens if you find a problem?

Urgent problems (active leak, AC out, signs of entry) get an immediate phone call plus photos. Non-urgent items are documented in your visit report. With your approval we can shut off water at the main, and we can meet your plumber or HVAC company at the home (vendor-meet add-on).

How do you get into my home?

By key, garage code or lockbox — arranged at the walkthrough. Keys and codes are stored securely and never labeled with your name or address.

How do I receive my reports?

By email after every visit: the date and time of the visit, each checklist item marked OK or flagged, photos, and notes on anything that needs attention.

Is Home Watch the same as a home inspection?

No. Home Watch is a recurring visual check of an empty home — it catches developing problems early. A home inspection (like our 11-month builder warranty inspection) is a one-time, in-depth evaluation under the TREC REI 7-6 standard.

Can I pause when I'm back in town?

Yes. Plans are month to month — pause while you're home and resume before your next trip. No long-term contract.
