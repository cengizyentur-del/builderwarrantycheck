Privacy Policy

BuilderWarrantyCheck mobile app — Last updated: October 22, 2026

The BuilderWarrantyCheck app is an inspection reporting tool for home inspectors. We designed it so that your inspection data stays under your control.

Data stored on your device

Inspections, findings, notes and photos you create in the app are stored locally on your device. BuilderWarrantyCheck does not operate accounts, and inspection reports are not uploaded to our servers.

Photos and the camera

The app uses the camera and photo library only so you can document property conditions and equipment data plates as part of your inspections. Photos remain on your device unless you choose to share a generated report.

Optional AI detail fill

The app offers an optional feature that reads equipment data-plate photos to fill in fields such as brand, year and capacity. This feature works only when you enter your own OpenAI API key in the app's Settings and tap the AI fill action. In that case, the photo you selected is sent to OpenAI for processing under OpenAI's privacy terms. No photo is sent anywhere unless you use this feature.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in
  • No advertising and no cross-app tracking
  • No sale of personal data
  • No third-party analytics SDKs collecting your inspection data

Reports you generate

PDF reports generated in the app are created on your device. If you share a report (for example by email or messages), that sharing happens through the app you choose and is governed by that service's policies.
